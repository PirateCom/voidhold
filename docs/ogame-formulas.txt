OGame formulas (merged)
Sources:
  https://ogame.fandom.com/wiki/Formulas
  https://punisher.forumotion.com/t13-ogame-formulas
  plus the later production, storage, and research formulas compared on 2026-09-22
Retrieved: 2026-09-22

S_E is the universe economy speed. It is 1 on a normal-speed universe.
S_F is the universe fleet speed. It is 1 on a normal-speed universe.
Resource-per-hour rates scale with S_E. Energy production and energy draw do not,
so a faster universe keeps the same energy balance and yields more resources.
Level 1 of a building costs its base cost. Costs are floored to integers.

Notation
  L = structure level being produced or currently active
  T = max temperature of the planet

================================================================================
MINES / POWER PLANTS
================================================================================

Production per hour
  Metal mine term       = round(30 * S_E * L * 1.1^L * (1 + 0.01 * Plasma_level))
  Crystal mine term     = round(20 * S_E * L * 1.1^L * (1 + 0.0066 * Plasma_level))
  Deuterium mine term   = round(10 * S_E * L * 1.1^L * (1.44 - 0.004 * T) * (1 + 0.0033 * Plasma_level))

  Metal per hour        = metal mine term + round(30 * S_E)
  Crystal per hour      = crystal mine term + round(15 * S_E)
  Deuterium per hour    = deuterium mine term

  The 30 metal and 15 crystal are the planet's base income. Plasma does not apply to them.
  Item and lifeform bonuses, when present, apply to the mine term.
  With Plasma at 0 and S_E at 1, metal is round(30 * L * 1.1^L) + 30
  and crystal is round(20 * L * 1.1^L) + 15.

  On a planet whose min and max temperatures differ by 40 degrees,
  (1.44 - 0.004 * T_max) equals (1.36 - 0.004 * average temperature).
  Colder planets produce more deuterium. The factor is 0 at 360 degrees max.

  Solar Plant energy        = 20 * L * 1.1^L
  Fusion Reactor energy     = 30 * L * (1.05 + Energy_Technology_level * 0.01)^L
  Solar Satellites          = floor(T / 4) + 20     energy per satellite

The pre-v0.78a fusion reactor produced 50 * L * 1.1^L energy and did not use Energy Technology.

Cost of one level
  cost = floor(base * factor^(L - 1))
  Cumulative cost from level 1 through L, before per-level flooring:
    base * (1 - factor^L) / (1 - factor)
  Flooring each level makes the true cumulative total slightly lower than that sum.

  Metal Mine                 factor 1.5    metal 60,    crystal 15
  Crystal Mine               factor 1.6    metal 48,    crystal 24
  Deuterium Synthesizer      factor 1.5    metal 225,   crystal 75
  Solar Plant                factor 1.5    metal 75,    crystal 30
  Fusion Reactor             factor 1.8    metal 900,   crystal 360,  deuterium 180
  Graviton Technology        factor 3
  Astrophysics               factor 1.75
  Every other building and research   factor 2

  Metal Mine level 10 costs floor(60 * 1.5^9) = 2306 metal
  and floor(15 * 1.5^9) = 576 crystal.

Consumption per hour
  Metal Mine energy             = 10 * L * 1.1^L
  Crystal Mine energy           = 10 * L * 1.1^L
  Deuterium Synthesizer energy  = 20 * L * 1.1^L
  Fusion Reactor deuterium      = round(10 * S_E * L * 1.1^L)

Storage capacity (metal, crystal, and deuterium storages)
  floor(2.5 * e^((20 / 33) * L)) * 5000
  Economy speed does not change capacity.
  Level 0 = 10000. Level 1 = 20000. Level 12 = 18005000.

================================================================================
PRODUCTION TIME
================================================================================

Result of each fraction is hours. Convert with
  seconds = max(1, floor(hours * 3600))
Each ship or defense unit takes at least 1 second. Deuterium cost is not part of build time.

Buildings
  (Metal + Crystal)
    / (2500 * (1 + Robotic_Factory_level) * 2^(Nanite_Factory_level) * S_E)

Ships and defense
  (Metal + Crystal)
    / (2500 * (1 + Shipyard_level) * 2^(Nanite_Factory_level) * S_E)
  Metal + Crystal is the unit's structural integrity.

Research
  (Metal + Crystal)
    / (1000 * (1 + Lab_sum) * S_E)
  Lab_sum is the lab on the planet doing the research, plus the Intergalactic
  Research Network level's worth of the highest labs on other planets.
  With IRN at 0, Lab_sum is that one lab.
  Nanite Factory does not change research time.

================================================================================
POINTS, PROTECTION, PLANET SIZE
================================================================================

Points
  1 point per 1000 resources spent on completed ships, buildings, research, and defense.
  Deuterium spent on construction and research counts.
  Deuterium spent as fuel, on a Sensor Phalanx scan, on a Fusion Reactor, or on viewing the galaxy does not count.
  Losing a unit or building removes the points it gave.
  Deconstruction grants no points. The points of the removed building are lost.

Separate rankings from the same era
  Research points = sum of all technology levels.
  Fleet points    = number of ships, of any type.

Noob protection (2008 brackets)
  Under 5000 points:
    You cannot attack a player above 500% of your points or below 20% of your points.
    Those players cannot attack you.
  From 5000 points upward:
    You cannot attack a player who is under 5000 points and below 20% of your points.
    That player cannot attack you. Anyone else can attack you, and you can attack them.
  From 25000 points upward:
    You can attack anyone above 5000 points, and they can attack you.

Universe
  9 galaxies. Each galaxy has systems 1 through 499.
  Each system has colonizable slots 1 through 15, plus an uncolonizable Outer Space slot.
  A new homeworld is usually in slots 4 through 12.
  90% of homeworlds roll there, 5% roll in slots 1 through 3, and 5% roll in slots 13 through 15.

Stars
  One star per solar system. It changes solar energy only. Temperatures stay on the slot table.
  Young hot star    solar plant and solar satellites ×1.5     25% of systems
  Medium star       ×1                                        50% of systems
  Old cold star     ×0.75                                     22% of systems
  Pulsar            ×3                                        3% of systems
  Mine energy draw does not change.

Temperature by slot
  Source: https://ogame.fandom.com/wiki/Temperature
  These are the base minimum and maximum. A planet then shifts both ends by the same
  integer from -10 to +10, so the span stays 40 degrees, 60 on slot 1, and 70 on slot 15.
  Slots 1–3 are hot. Slots 13–15 are cold.
  Slot   Min     Max
  1      200     260
  2      150     190
  3      100     140
  4       50      90
  5       40      80
  6       30      70
  7       20      60
  8       10      50
  9        0      40
  10     -10      30
  11     -20      20
  12     -30      10
  13     -70     -30
  14    -110     -70
  15    -180    -110

Planet fields by slot
  80% of planets roll inside the band. The other 20% roll in the next band of the same
  width just outside it, and the result is never below 20 fields.
  Slots 7–9 use 125 to 255. The label 200+/-45 does not describe that interval.
  Slots    80% of planets          Center
  1 to 3   40 to 70 fields         55+/-15
  4 to 6   120 to 310 fields       215+/-95
  7 to 9   125 to 255 fields       200+/-45
  10 to 12 75 to 125 fields        100+/-25
  13 to 15 60 to 190 fields        125+/-65

  Fields from diameter = floor((diameter_km / 1000)^2), rounded down.
  A 12,800 km world is floor(12.8^2) = 163 fields.
  Universe field bonus is added after that. The checked homeworld is 12,800 km and
  163 + 10 = 173 fields, shown as (used/173). Temperature still comes from the slot.
  Homeworlds use that fixed size. Other worlds roll a field count, then
  diameter_km = round(1000 * sqrt(max fields)).
  Each building level occupies one field.
  Terraformer extra fields = floor(5.5 * terraformer_level). Odd levels add 5, even levels add 6.
  The building occupies 1 field per level, so usable extra is extra fields minus level
  (4 at L1, 9 at L2, 45 at L10). Costs 0 metal, 50,000 crystal, 100,000 deuterium, 1,000 energy,
  doubling each level. Energy is total production at click, not leftover after mines.
  One free field is still required to start the upgrade.

Fields on a moon
  (diameter_in_km / 1000)^2

Fields usable on a moon
  1 + 3 * Lunar_Base_level
  The base itself occupies one field per level. The raw grant is 1 + 4 * level, and subtracting the occupied fields leaves 1 + 3 * level.

================================================================================
ESPIONAGE
================================================================================

Probes required
  probes = max(1, X + Y * (your_espionage - enemy_espionage)^2)
  Y = -1 when your Espionage Technology is higher
  Y =  1 when the target's Espionage Technology is higher
  Y =  0 when the levels are equal

  X = 2   ships
  X = 3   ships and defense
  X = 5   ships, defense, and buildings
  X = 7   ships, defense, buildings, and research
  Resources are shown no matter how many probes you send.
  A counter-espionage that destroys the probes still delivers the report.

Worked check, research view (X = 7), enemy level minus your level:
  -3 -> 1    -2 -> 3    -1 -> 6    0 -> 7    +1 -> 8    +2 -> 11    +3 -> 16

Incoming-fleet detail from your own Espionage Technology
  Level 2   number of ships
  Level 4   ship types as well
  Level 8   how many of each type

================================================================================
FLEET MOVEMENT
================================================================================

Distance
  Same planet, moon, or debris field          5
  Same system      1000 + 5 * |planet difference|
  Same galaxy      2700 + 95 * |system difference|
  Other galaxy     20000 * |galaxy difference|

Ship speed
  actual_speed = base_speed * (1 + drive_level * drive_bonus)
  drive_bonus = 0.1 combustion, 0.2 impulse, 0.3 hyperspace
  Fleet speed for the trip is the slowest ship in the fleet.

Flight time, in seconds
  round((35000 / speed_percent * sqrt(distance * 1000 / fleet_speed) + 10) / S_F)
  speed_percent is the chosen setting from 10 to 100.

Fuel for the flight, in deuterium
  1 + round(total_base_fuel * distance / 35000 * (speed_percent / 100 + 1)^2)
  total_base_fuel = sum over ships of (count * that ship's base fuel use)
  This matches a fleet whose ships share one speed. The 2008 post does not specify mixed-speed fleets.

Deuterium per hour while holding for an ACS defend or an expedition
  Small Cargo        5
  Large Cargo        5
  Light Fighter      2
  Heavy Fighter      7
  Cruiser            30
  Battleship         50
  Colony Ship        100
  Recycler           30
  Espionage Probe    0.1
  Bomber             100
  Solar Satellite    none
  Destroyer          100
  Deathstar          0.1
  Battlecruiser      25

Interplanetary missiles
  Range in systems = Impulse_Drive_level * 5 - 1
  Flight time = 30 seconds, plus 60 seconds for every system crossed
  Missiles cannot cross galaxies.

Expeditions
  Concurrent expeditions = floor(sqrt(Astrophysics_level))
  Hold time is a whole number of hours from 1 up to the technology level.
  Astrophysics is the later name of Expedition Technology. Its cost factor is 1.75.

================================================================================
MOON, PHALANX, PLUNDER
================================================================================

Chance to create a moon
  Percent = debris / 100000, and the listed ship formula uses a 30% debris rate.
  Ships of one type for an X% chance
    = (100000 * X) / ((metal + crystal of one ship) * 0.3)

Moon destruction
  Chance the moon is destroyed, in percent
    = (100 - sqrt(Moonsize)) * sqrt(number of Death Stars)
  Chance the entire attacking fleet is destroyed, in percent
    = sqrt(Moonsize) / 2

Sensor Phalanx
  Range in systems = Phalanx_level^2 - 1

  Scanning the origin planet shows:
    outbound fleets except deployment
    fleets on the return leg
    recalled fleets except recalled deployment
    The time shown is the time left in the mission.
  Scanning the target planet shows inbound fleets.
  Hidden from a target scan:
    recalled deployments
    fleets going to that planet's moon or debris field
    fleets on the return leg
  Hidden from an origin scan:
    recalled deployments

Plunder
  Default cap is 50% of each resource, which is also 50% of the planet's total.
  A universe or target class can raise that same per-resource cap to 75% or 100%.
  Example at 50%: 20000 metal, 20000 crystal, 10000 deuterium can yield at most 25000 resources.

  Fill cargo in this order, stopping at each resource's cap:
    1. Metal, up to 1/3 of cargo.
    2. Crystal, up to half of the cargo still empty.
    3. Deuterium, with whatever cargo is still empty.
    4. If cargo remains, metal for half of what remains.
    5. Crystal for the rest.
